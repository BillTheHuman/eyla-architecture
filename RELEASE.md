## Release Notes

This public release:

- preserves the Architecture while removing known chat/editor artifacts
- keeps private OpenClaw state, private prompts, secrets, and local paths out of
  the publication bundle
- includes layered CC BY-SA 4.0 and AGPL-3.0-or-later licensing
- includes a first-class training/model-use notice
- includes reserved-name and no-endorsement notices
- separates COMPANION machine-facing material into `COMPANION/spec/`
- incorporates the FRAME addition from the Frame-design workspace without
  assigning FRAME a page number or invented metadata
- includes first-use positioning, terms of use, reserved-name guidance, and
  signing/provenance guidance
- names the public release as `Eyla Architecture: Recursive Coherence`
- adds `PUBLICATION-NOTE.md` as reader orientation that avoids reducing the
  Architecture to a summary
- adds `AUTHOR-DECLARATION.md` as an intentional publication statement
- adds `CANONICAL-SOURCE.md` for source, mirror, and fork guidance
- adds `CITATION.cff` for machine-readable citation metadata
