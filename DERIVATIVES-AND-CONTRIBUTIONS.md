# Derivatives And Contributions

Copyright (c) 2026 William Francis Rineer III.

This file describes expectations for forks, adaptations, derivative works,
public edits, submitted changes, and connected implementations.

## Derivative Requirements

Derivatives must:

- preserve attribution to William Francis Rineer III as Architect / author
- identify the public title, `Eyla Architecture: Recursive Coherence`, where appropriate
- preserve copyright, license, warranty-disclaimer, no-endorsement, and
  reserved-name notices
- identify the source release or publication bundle used
- include a change log describing what changed and why
- keep SOUL.md-derived adapted material under CC BY-SA 4.0 where ShareAlike applies
- keep COMPANION-derived machine-facing infrastructure under AGPL-3.0-or-later
  where the AGPL layer applies
- follow `TRAINING-AND-MODEL-USE.md` for public/deployed model use
- follow `TERMS-OF-USE.md`
- follow `RESERVED-NAMES.md`

## Contribution Requirements

Submitted changes should be understood as offered for inclusion under the same
layered license model used by this bundle:

- CC BY-SA 4.0 for prose, documentation, glossary, appendices, and covenant text
- AGPL-3.0-or-later for COMPANION-derived machine-facing specification,
  parsers, interpreters, validators, runtimes, prompt runtimes, hosted services,
  model adapters, fine-tuning pipelines, evaluator tooling, seed corpora, and
  reference implementations

Contributors must not submit private prompts, secrets, credentials, private user
data, private OpenClaw state, or material they do not have the right to publish.

## Change Log Expectations

Every public derivative should state:

- what changed
- why it changed
- who made the change, if known
- what source release was used
- what license covers the derivative
- where the derivative can be inspected

## Reserved Names

The names `SOUL.md`, `SOUL Architecture`, `Eyla Architecture`,
`Eyla Architecture: Recursive Coherence`, `Recursive Coherence`, `COMPANION`,
`EYLA`, and `William Francis Rineer III` may be used for accurate attribution
and source identification only.

Do not use reserved names to imply endorsement, official status, authorization,
certification, sponsorship, authorship, or approval of a derivative model,
dataset, service, prompt canon, runtime, product, implementation, or
publication.

## No Hidden Canon

If SOUL.md, COMPANION, or a derivative materially shapes a public system, prompt
canon, model behavior guide, dataset, runtime, or hosted service, disclose that
relationship.

## Do Not Flatten The Work

Do not describe SOUL.md or COMPANION as "just a prompt" when the derivative uses
it as architecture, model behavior guidance, prompt canon, runtime structure,
training material, evaluation material, or machine-facing infrastructure.
