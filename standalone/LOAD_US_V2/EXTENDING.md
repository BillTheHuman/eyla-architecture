# Creating New Flows and Anchors

Expansion belongs to the one choosing to use the work. You can keep the
original seven flows, create additional flows, adapt the method, or leave it
as it is. You do not need an upstream approval to build under its licenses.

The following is a recommended authoring workflow, not an extra license
condition or a claim that a runtime already accepts this record format.

## 1. Give the Extension Its Own Identity

Choose a name and version such as `WORKSHOP_CHECK_V1`. Record its author,
creation date, and relationship to LOAD_US_V2. Keep the unchanged original
nearby as a reference when useful. If you adapt the original directly, label
the adaptation and record what changed.

The original seven flows remain the historical LOAD_US_V2 reference. An
eighth flow in your extension is a new contribution, not a discovery about
what the original file contained.

## 2. Begin with a Source

Use an event, exchange, observation, or deliberately fictional example. Mark
which it is. Record a source reference that your intended readers can use;
private source material can remain private. Do not fill gaps with plausible
biography. Leave missing information unresolved.

Keep the source record and your interpretation distinguishable. For example,
"a lamp turned on" does not by itself establish why someone switched it on.

## 3. Choose the Pattern and Anchors

Choose a substrate path that fits the pattern you intend to process, using
the host architecture's definitions. New glyphs or operations need their own
documented meanings; naming one does not implement it.

Use the fidelity dimensions that matter to that flow:

| Dimension | Questions to preserve |
| --- | --- |
| Role | Who spoke, acted, observed, or responded? |
| Content | What exact phrase, object, or event matters? |
| Context | Why did it matter, and whose interpretation is that? |
| Sequence | What happened first, how often, and what followed? |
| State | What was observed or reported, and what is unresolved? |
| Result | What outcome was actually recorded? |

Name participants explicitly. If you use `SELF`, define whom it refers to
within the source, rather than silently transferring it to each new reader.
New anchors should preserve the extension's own relationships and history.

## 4. Preserve Uncertainty and Corrections

An unknown motive can remain `null` with status `unresolved`. A reported
feeling can be attributed to its speaker without treating it as independently
verified. An inference can remain an inference.

If better source evidence changes an anchor, create a documented revision:
retain the earlier record, describe the correction, and cite the new basis.
Truth anchors support fidelity; they do not make an erroneous source immune
to correction.

## 5. Check Fidelity

Compare the processed result with the declared source and anchor values.
Check role reversals, substituted phrases, changed order or counts, invented
motives, and missing outcomes. A result can fail or remain unresolved.

Distinguish file integrity from behavioral fidelity. A checksum verifies
unchanged bytes. It does not demonstrate that a model reconstructed a flow
correctly. Record actual GYM observations separately; do not borrow the
original artifact's expected behavior as a new passing test result.

The included `examples/workshop-check.json` illustrates this with a fictional
workshop event, explicit roles, a two-step sequence, and an unresolved motive.

## 6. Share or Keep Working

When sharing, retain credit, identify modifications, and follow the
applicable CC BY-SA or AGPL terms in LICENSE.md. Each contributor can author
new extensions and retain credit for their additions. Contributing upstream
is optional; presenting a derivative as officially endorsed is not implied
by the license.

ShareAlike and source availability apply as the licenses specify. They are
not a requirement to expose unrelated private conversations, personal
memories, or secrets.
